BATCH 8b – OUTPOSTS                          [SKIPPED → TEXEL]
================================================================

After 2 dedicated re-runs (pass 2 budget 200, pass 3 budget 400),
sigma remains 0.18-0.24. The fundamental cause is structural:
outpost positions (a piece in the opponent's half on a square that
cannot be challenged by an enemy pawn, protected by a friendly pawn)
arise in perhaps 5-10% of positions from ogpt_chess_startpos.epd
games. With ~80 games per evaluation and 10% outpost frequency, each
eval contains ~320 outpost decisions on average — far too few to
separate the signal from game result noise.

Further SPSA investment will not fix this regardless of budget.
A positional EPD (closed structures, longer games, more maneuvering)
would help, but then search and tactics parameters would need
retuning on that book too, creating an inconsistency.

Correct approach: Texel tuning on a large quiet-position dataset.
Outpost positions at all frequencies will appear naturally in 10M+
positions, and the gradient is computed analytically rather than
estimated from game results. Use the following rough theory priors
as Texel starting points (not the noisy SPSA values):

  Knight_Outpost: MG ~18, EG ~10
  Bishop_Outpost: MG ~10, EG ~12
  Rook_Outpost:   MG ~25, EG ~15
  Queen_Outpost:  MG ~ 6, EG ~ 6
