Specifically: search params (LMR, RFP, FP) interact tightly with each other,
so tune them first in isolation. Once your eval params shift in later batches,
your LMR base may no longer be optimal — that's why Batch 1 gets a re-run after
all eval batches complete. The final pass then locks everything in together. You
never tune each param exactly once; the convergence is iterative. For Lakas
specifically, ~3k–5k games per batch is usually enough to see convergence on the
high-impact params; the king safety and threat arrays need more like 6k–8k due to
lower signal frequency.

Strategy overview
-----------------
DO NOT tune all params at once – SPSA gradient estimates get swamped
by noise from too many simultaneous perturbations.

Recommended flow:
  1. Run each batch below (~3 000–6 000 games per batch).
  2. After all batches complete, re-run Batch 1 (search params can
     drift once eval params shift).
  3. Run a FINAL PASS with every param that moved > 5% from its
     initial value, all together (~10 000+ games).
  4. PSTs are NOT tuned here – use a Texel / gradient tuner instead.

Lakas C values (perturbation size) guidance:
  Integer params  : c = max(1, round(0.002 * |initial|))
  LMR_Base/Scale  : c = 0.05
