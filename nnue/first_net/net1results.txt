Net1 methodology
================

Goal: first working Chess768 NNUE bootstrap from SHAYVERI self-play data.

Data:
- About 1.0B positions total.
- 40 chunks of 25M positions.
- Generated from the HCE engine before NNUE self-play was trusted.
- Plain Marlinflow format: FEN | centipawn score from White POV | WDL.

Training:
- Marlinflow trainer.
- Chess768 input, 256 hidden units, single scalar output.
- Mixed score/WDL target from the first working training pipeline.
- Converted final Marlinflow JSON/checkpoint into first_net.nnue.

Important fixes made during/after this net:
- Corrected Chess768 feature indexing to match Marlinflow Board768.
- Corrected side-to-move / non-side-to-move accumulator ordering.
- Corrected quantization scales in the Marlinflow-to-NNUE converter.
- Tuned eval scale until bench output looked sane.

Estimated Elo relative to HCE
=============================

Best estimate for net1 / curr: about -50 Elo vs HCE.

Reasoning:
- Direct HCE matches put the early NNUE around -30 to -50 Elo.
- The net1 checkpoint chosen as curr looked better than some earlier/incomplete NNUE builds, but still did not clearly beat HCE.
- Later HCE tests were noisy and sometimes used different checkpoints/settings, so -50 is the safer anchor.
- Treat the estimate as rough, around +/- 40 Elo.
