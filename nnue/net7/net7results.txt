Net7 methodology
================

Goal: fine-tune from the net5 winner with a strongly HCE-heavy mix.

Baseline
========

Selected checkpoint: net5 iter064.

Direct net5 iter064 strength vs HCE:
- 10+0.1: +236.8 +/- 24.5 Elo
- 90+0.5: +220.1 +/- 42.5 Elo

Anchored net5 iter064 estimate from Elo.md:
- 10+0.1: 2889.5 +/- 27.1
- 90+0.5: 2954.6 +/- 47.1

Why net7 exists
===============

Net6 tested a controlled 50/50 HCE/NNUE mix and failed immediately:

Score of iter009 vs curr: 177 - 248 - 375  [0.456] 800
   iter009 playing White: 110 - 103 - 187  [0.509] 400
   iter009 playing Black:  67 - 145 - 188  [0.403] 400
          White vs Black: 255 - 170 - 375  [0.553] 800
Elo difference: -30.9 +/- 17.5, LOS: 0.0 %, DrawRatio: 46.9 %

The lesson is that NNUE self-data helps, but too much of it drifts away from the
net5 optimum. Net7 keeps NNUE data as a minority signal and lets HCE data anchor
the distribution.

Planned data
============

- Shared-disk multi-worker setup.
- 5 workers.
- 5M positions per chunk.
- 40 chunks per worker unless stopped early.
- 1B total positions if all chunks complete.
- 10k-node labels.
- WDL=0.1.
- Same Chess768, hidden=256 Marlinflow trainer.
- Resume from the net5 iter064 PyTorch checkpoint.

Planned HCE/NNUE split
======================

Target split:
- 80% HCE-generated data.
- 20% NNUE-generated data.
- Every 10 iterations is guaranteed to be 8 HCE / 2 NNUE.

Implementation:
- Workers generate two NNUE chunks followed by eight HCE chunks in each 10-chunk cycle.
- Worker chunk directories are prefixed with source: `nnue_...` or `hce_...`.
- Each chunk writes `source=nnue` or `source=hce` in metadata.
- The master claims chunks by source slot:
  - iterations 0-1 in each 10-iteration block require NNUE chunks.
  - iterations 2-9 in each 10-iteration block require HCE chunks.
- If the required source is not ready, the master waits instead of consuming the
  wrong source.

Training plan
=============

This is a fine-tune, not another high-LR continuation run.

Planned defaults:
- LR: 0.00003
- LR drop: 0.00001 after 100 chunks
- second LR drop: 0.00001 after 200 chunks
- epochs per chunk: 1
- batch size: 16384
- scale: 400
- save every epoch

Pre-run checklist
=================

- Net7 scripts use v2.6 names.
- Use NFS root `~/nnue_v2_6`.
- Use train id `shayveri_v2.6`.
- Set `RESUME` to the net5 iter064 checkpoint.
- Ensure `NET_DIR` contains the selected net5 `.nnue` at the default eval path.
- Ensure `NO_NET_DIR` contains only the engine binary and no default `.nnue`.
- Confirm `./SHAYVERI datagen <threads> <positions> <prefix> <nodes>` still has no
  eval-file argument.
- Confirm chunk metadata writes `source=nnue` or `source=hce`.
- Confirm first 10 done chunks contain 2 NNUE and 8 HCE.

Checkpoint testing plan
=======================

Primary:
- Test `iter009`, `iter019`, and `iter029` directly against net5 iter064.

Kill condition:
- If `iter009` is worse than net5 iter064 by more than about 20 Elo over 800 games,
  stop immediately.

If early signal is good:
- Continue to 50-80 chunks.
- Test every 10th checkpoint.
- Play off the best candidates.
- Confirm the winner directly against net5 iter064 and HCE at 10+0.1.
- If it is clearly stronger, run a 90+0.5 match vs HCE.

Expected signal
===============

Reasonable target:
- +20 to +50 Elo over net5 iter064.

Great outcome:
- +70 Elo or more over net5 iter064.

Failure signal:
- Early checkpoints repeat net6 behavior: black performance collapses, or the first
  completed 10-chunk batch loses clearly to net5 iter064.

Conclusion
==========

Net7 failed as a mainline continuation.

The HCE-heavy 80/20 mix is better than net6's 50/50 mix, but it still does not beat
net5 iter064. The direct iter009 result is the cleanest signal: even the first
controlled 10-chunk batch moves away from the net5 optimum.

Current best remains:
- net5 iter064
- estimated 10+0.1 rating: 2889.5 +/- 27.1
- estimated 90+0.5 rating: 2954.6 +/- 47.1

Lesson
======

More resumed training from net5 iter064 is not automatically useful. With the current
Marlinflow trainer, Chess768 architecture, and LR range, both controlled 50/50 and
controlled 80/20 continuation runs regress immediately.

The problem is likely not just data ratio. It may be one or more of:
- resume fine-tuning LR still too high for a sharp checkpoint.
- net5 iter064 was a local optimum created by noisy chunk order.
- stronger/self-generated NNUE data narrows or biases the distribution.
- Chess768/256 is near its useful ceiling under this trainer setup.

Recommended next experiment
===========================

Do not continue net7.
Architectural changes to be made with net8.
