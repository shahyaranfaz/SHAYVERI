Net10 methodology
=================

Goal: respond to net9's long-time-control failure by running a more conservative
KB8 continuation from the last trusted checkpoint, net8 iter029.

Baseline
========

Current trusted KB8 checkpoint:
- net8 iter029 / `net8_final.nnue`.
- Selected over net8 iter049 by:
  `Score of iter029 vs iter049: 345 - 274 - 181 [0.544] 800`
  for +30.9 +/- 21.2 Elo.
- Direct net8 vs net5:
  - 10+0.1: +25.6 +/- 20.1 Elo over net5.
  - 90+0.5: +69.6 +/- 67.1 Elo over net5, small sample.

Why net10 exists
================

Net9 fixed the pipeline problems from the net8 tail:
- 1M chunks reduced queue/disk pressure.
- Bullet conversion/shuffle validation caught bad chunks.
- Resume path handling was fixed.
- `BATCHES_PER_SUPERBATCH` is now computed from actual chunk size.
- UCI/search lifecycle bugs causing illegal moves were fixed.

But net9 itself failed as a strength continuation:

Score of iter039 vs curr at 10+0.1:
`20 - 58 - 39 [0.338] 117`, -117.1 +/- 53.2 Elo.

The split was the important part:
- White: `18 - 6 - 33 [0.605]`.
- Black: `2 - 52 - 6 [0.083]`.

Fast 0.5+0.05 testing made iter039 look plausible, but 10+0.1 exposed a severe
Black-side/generalization collapse. Net10 therefore restarted from net8, not
from net9.

Net10 changes from net9
=======================

- Restart from net8 iter029.
- Keep KB8 architecture.
- Keep Bullet trainer and strict `convert_bullet.py` format.
- Keep 1M chunks and all master validation/disk-safety fixes.
- Make the source mix more conservative:
  - net9 used 80% HCE / 20% NNUE.
  - net10 uses 90% HCE / 10% NNUE.
- Lower the continuation LR:
  - net9 used `LR=0.001`.
  - net10 uses `LR=0.0003`.
  - drop to `0.0001` after 100 chunks.
  - drop to `0.00003` after 200 chunks.

Reasoning
=========

Net9's pipeline was valid but its training recipe drifted away from the net8
balance. The 10+0.1 result was not a small generic loss; it was a color split
collapse. That points to distribution/training drift more than architecture.

Net10 tests a smaller update step and a stronger HCE anchor:
- 90/10 keeps some NNUE self-play signal.
- HCE-heavy data should reduce narrow self-play bias.
- Lower LR should preserve the net8 checkpoint instead of rapidly overwriting it.

Setup
=====

- NFS root: `~/nnue_v2_9`.
- Train id: `shayveri_v2.9_kb8`.
- Resume checkpoint: net8 iter029 Bullet checkpoint,
  `~/nnue_v2_7/out/shayveri_v2.7_kb8_iter029-1`.
- `POSITIONS=1000000`.
- `NODES=10000`.
- `WDL=0.1`.
- `SCALE=400`.
- `BATCH_SIZE=16384`.
- `EPOCHS=1`.
- `LR=0.0003`.
- `LR_AFTER_DROP=0.0001` at chunk 100.
- `SECOND_LR_AFTER_DROP=0.00003` at chunk 200.
- Default worker `SOURCE_PATTERN=NHHHHHHHHH`.
- Master schedule: each 10-iteration block consumes 1 NNUE chunk then 9 HCE
  chunks.

Conclusion
==========

Net10 is a real improvement at 10+0.1 and a wash at 90+0.5 so far.

The cleanest result is the 2000-game 10+0.1 match: iter010 is +38.7 +/- 12.3 Elo
against curr/net8, with balanced White and Black scores. That specifically fixes
net9's failure mode, where the candidate looked okay in fast pools but collapsed
as Black at longer time control.

The 90+0.5 match is not a promotion-strength result yet. It says iter010 is
roughly equal to curr at LTC, with high uncertainty and a strange color split.
The sample is large enough to reject a huge LTC gain, but not large enough to
separate small gains from noise.

Net10's main lesson is that the useful update happened extremely early, around
iter001 to iter010. Continuing past that did not help and usually hurt.

Estimated rating
================

These estimates are anchored through existing HCE measurements, so they should be
treated as approximate pool-relative ratings, not universal Elo.

Known anchors:
- HCE at 10+0.1: 2652.7.
- HCE at 90+0.5: 2734.5.
- net5 iter064 vs HCE:
  - +236.8 +/- 24.5 at 10+0.1.
  - +220.1 +/- 42.5 at 90+0.5.
- net8/curr vs net5:
  - +25.6 +/- 20.1 at 10+0.1.
  - +69.6 +/- 67.1 at 90+0.5, small sample.
- net10 iter010 vs curr:
  - +38.7 +/- 12.3 at 10+0.1.
  - -2.5 +/- 31.2 at 90+0.5.

Estimated net10 iter010 rating:
- 10+0.1: about 2954 Elo.
- 90+0.5: about 3022 Elo, but with very high uncertainty.

Confidence:
- STC estimate is fairly solid for this project, roughly +/- 35 Elo.
- LTC estimate is loose, roughly +/- 90 Elo, because both the net8-vs-net5 LTC
  anchor and the net10-vs-curr LTC match are small/noisy.

Practical interpretation:
- At 10+0.1, net10 iter010 is probably the strongest SHAYVERI net so far.
- At 90+0.5, net10 iter010 is probably comparable to net8/curr, not clearly
  better yet.
