Net5 methodology
================

Goal: extend the net3 gains with stronger self-generated data and test how much Elo
remains in the current Marlinflow setup and Chess768 architecture.

Data differences from net3:
- Kept the shared-disk multi-worker setup.
- 1B positions: 40 chunks per worker * 5 workers * 5M positions.
- Kept WDL=0.1.
- Back to 10k-node labels.
- No architecture or trainer changes.
- Datagen uses 60% old HCE (3 PCs) and 40% NNUE (2 PCs).
- Training resumes from the net3 iter240 PyTorch checkpoint.
