Net5 methodology
================

Goal: extend the net3 gains with stronger self-generated data and test how much Elo
remains in the current Marlinflow setup and Chess768 architecture.

Data differences from net3:
- Kept the shared-disk multi-worker setup.
- 1B positions: 40 chunks per worker * 5 workers * 5M positions.
- Kept WDL=0.1.
- Back to 10k-node labels.
- No architecture or trainer changes.
- Datagen uses 60% old HCE (3 PCs) and 40% NNUE (2 PCs).
- Training resumes from the net3 iter240 PyTorch checkpoint.

Result:
- Net5 succeeded. The current winner is iter064.
- iter064 beat iter037 directly by +30.9 +/- 17.0 Elo at 1+0.1.
- In the final 10+0.1 pool, iter064 finished clearly ahead of both curr/net3 and HCE:
  iter064 141, curr 44, HCE -194.
- Direct 10+0.1 match vs HCE: +236.8 +/- 24.5 Elo.
- Direct 90+0.5 match vs HCE: +220.1 +/- 42.5 Elo.
- Using the HCE anchors in Elo.md, current estimated ratings are:
  - 10+0.1: 2889.5 +/- 27.1
  - 90+0.5: 2954.6 +/- 47.1
- Main lesson for net6: the mixed HCE/NNUE data worked, but the master consumed chunks
  in arrival order. Because NNUE workers finish faster, iteration-to-iteration strength
  became noisy and non-monotonic. Net6 should enforce a balanced chunk schedule.

Illegal PV warning
==================

Cutechess reported:

Warning: Illegal PV move b1d2info from iter064 (1377)
Warning: PV: O-O c3 g6 b1d2info

This looks like a PV-output problem rather than a confirmed illegal bestmove. The likely cause is
that the engine followed transposition-table best moves while printing the PV and appended the move
before checking whether it was legal in the current PV line. A stale/collided TT move could therefore
be printed in `info pv`, which cutechess then flags even if the actual `bestmove` is legal.

Fix: PV printing now calls `make_move` before appending each PV move. Illegal TT continuations are
silently truncated instead of printed.
