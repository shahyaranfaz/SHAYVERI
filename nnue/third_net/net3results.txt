Net3 methodology
================

Goal: fix the net2 recipe while keeping the distributed worker/master pipeline.

Differences from net2:
- Kept the shared-disk multi-worker setup.
- Planned about 1.25B positions: 50 chunks per worker * 5 workers * 5M positions.
- Restored WDL signal with WDL=0.1 instead of effectively CP-only training.
- Kept 10k-node labels.
- Added stronger early-game coverage: book-guided openings and saved early quiet positions.
- Kept Chess768/256 architecture for a clean recipe test before adding king buckets or Bullet.

Result:
- Net3 iter050 was already basically equal to curr:
  curr vs net3_iter050: 404 - 396 - 200 [0.504] 1000, curr +2.8 +/- 19.3.
- Net3 iter150 became clearly stronger than curr inside the checkpoint gauntlet.

Estimated Elo relative to HCE:
- If curr / net1 is estimated around -50 vs HCE, net3 iter150 is about +100 vs HCE.
- This is bridged through curr: iter150 is roughly +151 over curr in the table below.
- Treat as approximate until a direct net3-vs-HCE gauntlet confirms it.
