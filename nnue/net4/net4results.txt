Net4 methodology
================

Goal: extend the net3 gains with stronger self-generated data and test how much Elo
remains in the current Marlinflow setup and Chess768 architecture.

Data differences from net3:
- Kept the shared-disk multi-worker setup.
- 1.25B positions: 50 chunks per worker * 5 workers * 5M positions.
- Kept WDL=0.1.
- 15k-node labels.
- No architecture or trainer changes.
- Datagen uses the stronger net3 eval.
- Training resumes from the net3 iter240 PyTorch checkpoint.

Result:
- Initial attempt accidentally started from random weights because the net3 checkpoint directory had been deleted on the training machine. This produced extremely weak early checkpoints and was discarded.
- The run was restarted after transferring the net3 iter240 checkpoint back to the trainer and setting RESUME=.../shayveri_v2.2_iter240_1.
- The run was restarted again with lower LR
- The run was abandoned after 61 iterations (305M Positions).
