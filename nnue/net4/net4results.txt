Net4 methodology
================

Goal: Extend on net3 wins, using better data, try and squeeze marlinflow dry of Elo (in however many runs).

Data differences from net3:
- Kept the shared-disk multi-worker setup.
- 750M positions: 50 chunks per worker * 3 workers * 5M positions.
- WDL signal with WDL=0.1 instead of effectively CP-only training.
- 15k-node labels.
- No changes to codebase, except use of much stronger net3's eval.
