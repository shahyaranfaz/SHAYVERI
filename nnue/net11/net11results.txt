Net11 methodology
=================

Goal: decide whether KB8 still has useful continuation gains after net10, or
whether the next real improvement requires a larger architecture.

Baseline
========

Current best checkpoint:
- net10 iter010.
- Selected because it validated directly against curr/net8 at 10+0.1:

Score of iter010 vs curr: 757 - 535 - 708  [0.555] 2000
   iter010 playing White: 353 - 249 - 398  [0.552] 1000
   iter010 playing Black: 404 - 286 - 310  [0.559] 1000
          White vs Black: 639 - 653 - 708  [0.496] 2000
Elo difference: +38.7 +/- 12.3

LTC check:

Score of iter010 vs curr: 80 - 82 - 116  [0.496] 278
Elo difference: -2.5 +/- 31.2

So net10 iter010 is a real STC improvement and roughly equal at LTC so far.

Why net11 exists
================

The KB8 project has mostly delivered the hoped-for gain:
- net5 iter064 was +236.8 Elo vs HCE at 10+0.1.
- net8 added about +25.6 Elo over net5 at 10+0.1.
- net10 iter010 added +38.7 Elo over net8/curr at 10+0.1.

That puts net10 around +300 Elo vs HCE at 10+0.1, roughly +60 to +70 Elo above
the old Chess768/256 champion. This is close to the original +80 Elo hope for
KB8, depending on anchor noise.

But the training behavior is unstable:
- net8 had a useful early/mid peak, then the tail became contaminated.
- net9 trained legally after fixes but drifted badly and collapsed at 10+0.1,
  especially as Black.
- net10 improved sharply very early, around iter001-iter010, then later chunks
  became worse or noisy.

Net11 should therefore not be "more chunks and hope." It should test whether a
tiny consolidation step from net10 iter010 can add a small amount without
destroying the balance.

Net11 plan
==========

Starting point:
- Resume from net10 iter010.
- Do not resume from net8.
- Do not resume from later net10 checkpoints.
- Keep net10 iter001 as a backup reference because it tied iter010 in the small
  playoff, but iter010 is the main parent because it has the direct 10+0.1 win.

Training hypothesis:
- KB8 is not completely maxed out, because net10 found +38.7 Elo at 10+0.1.
- The current recipe cannot absorb many chunks cleanly.
- The value is probably in very small updates, not long continuation.
- Net11 should be a low-LR consolidation run, not a full-length training run.

Recommended recipe:
- Same KB8 architecture.
- Same Bullet trainer and `convert_bullet.py` format.
- Same 1M chunk pipeline and validation.
- Resume from net10 iter010.
- Use very low LR:
  - `LR=0.00003`
  - `LR_AFTER_DROP=0.00001` after 100 chunks
  - `SECOND_LR_AFTER_DROP=0.00001` after 200 chunks
- Keep HCE-dominant data:
  - 95% HCE / 5% NNUE
  - possible side test: 100% HCE for 10 chunks if drift continues
- Keep `WDL=0.1`, `SCALE=400`, `BATCH_SIZE=16384`, `EPOCHS=1`.

Script defaults:
- NFS root: `~/nnue_v2_10`.
- Train id: `shayveri_v2.10_kb8`.
- Initial resume:
  `~/nnue_v2_9/out/shayveri_v2.9_kb8_iter010-1`.
- Worker source pattern: `NHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH`.
- Master source schedule: one NNUE chunk followed by nineteen HCE chunks in
  each 20-iteration block.

Testing plan
============

Do not wait for 100 chunks.

Primary scan:
- Convert/test every checkpoint from iter000 through iter014.
- Include net10 iter010 in the pool.
- Use the same 0.5+0.05 settings as previous checkpoint scans.

Promotion tests:
- Any promising candidate must beat net10 iter010 directly at 10+0.1.
- Only after that, test 90+0.5.
- Track White/Black split explicitly; do not trust aggregate Elo alone.

Kill conditions:
- If the first 10-15 chunks do not beat or tie net10 iter010, stop.
- If STC improves but color split becomes ugly, reject the candidate.
- If LTC remains equal while STC improves, keep the STC winner but do not claim
  LTC improvement.

Decision after net11
====================

If net11 finds another +10 to +25 Elo at 10+0.1 without hurting LTC:
- Promote it.
- Then run one more tiny consolidation only if the curve still looks healthy.

If net11 is flat or worse:
- Stop squeezing KB8/256 continuation.
- Move to architecture work for the next major run.

Next architecture options:
- KB16 with similar hidden size, if speed remains acceptable.
- Wider KB8, such as hidden 384 or 512, if eval speed budget allows it.
- Add richer features only after confirming the current KB8 speed/strength trade
  is exhausted.

Expected outcome
================

Reasonable target:
- +10 to +25 Elo over net10 iter010 at 10+0.1.

Great outcome:
- +30 Elo at 10+0.1 while staying equal or better at 90+0.5.

Most likely:
- Small STC gain or no gain.
- LTC gain is less likely from simple continuation; it may need a larger net or
  a data distribution change.

Conclusion
==========

Net11 failed as a continuation.

The low-LR 95/5 consolidation idea did not improve net10 iter010. Early samples
had a few checkpoints near net10, but every larger follow-up put net10 back on
top. By 172 iterations, the result was clear enough to stop: later checkpoints
were not only failing to improve, they were drifting away.

Current best remains:
- net10 iter010.

Main lesson:
- KB8/256 has likely given most of its easy continuation gain.
- More conservative continuation from net10 does not solve the drift problem.
- The next serious strength attempt should be architectural, not another long
  KB8/256 fine-tune.

Recommended next experiment:
- Move to a larger or richer architecture, most likely KB16 or a wider KB8.
- Keep the fixed Bullet/1M validation pipeline.
- Keep early dense checkpoint testing, because useful peaks appear very early
  and later training can regress.
